Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · updated November 2026 (optional online features) · Retkol: Live Wallpapers (app.retkol.wallpapers)

In short
  • Retkol has no account, no ads, no analytics libraries and no tracking. Every scene works offline.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it. The online features are all optional and all off until you turn them on.
  • If you order your own cat, its photos are used only to paint it and are deleted once you say it's your cat (after 7 days at most).
  • We never sell or share personal data, and never use it for advertising.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Who we are

Retkol is made by Ibtisam (IbtiStudio), an independent developer in India. If you have any question about this policy or your privacy, write to reach.ibtisam@gmail.com.

What the app uses, and why

Retkol is a live wallpaper. Everything a scene does happens on your phone, and every scene works without an internet connection. The app connects to the internet only for the optional features described under "Online features" below, and then only to Retkol's own server.

  • Approximate location (optional). If you choose to allow it in Settings, the app takes one approximate reading so the sun, the moon and the seasons in the scenes match your real sky. If you don't, it estimates your region from your phone's time zone instead. The reading is kept only on your phone and is never transmitted.
  • Step count (optional). If you allow physical-activity access, the Walk Garden scene reads your phone's own step counter so the garden grows as you walk. The count is used and kept only on your phone, is never transmitted, and is never used for advertising. The scene works without it.
  • Vibration. Used for small taps of feedback in some scenes. It involves no data.
  • Your choices and progress. The scene you picked, your settings and each scene's progress (for example, how your garden has grown) are saved on your phone so they're there tomorrow.

Online features (all optional)

Each of these is off until you turn it on (in Settings › Online) or use it. When you do, your phone talks only to Retkol's own server, which runs on Cloudflare (our hosting provider). The server never receives your name, email, phone number or Google account. To tell phones apart it uses a random key your phone makes the first time it goes online; it isn't linked to you or your phone's identifiers.

  • Your own cat (a paid order). If you order a coat made from your cat, the one or two photos you choose are sent to our server, made smaller first and with their hidden details (such as where they were taken) removed. They are used only to paint your cat: our painting tool runs on our own computer and uses OpenAI's image service to draw it. The photos are deleted from our server once you say the painted cat is yours, and 7 days after you send them whatever happens; our own copies are deleted as soon as each painting is made. We keep the finished coat (not the photos) so you can get it back on a new phone. To check the order was paid for, the server checks Google Play's receipt for that purchase.
  • Friends. If you pair with a friend's phone by a code, the small things you send them (a knock on the window, a drawing, a move in a game, a note, a visit) pass through our server to them and are deleted after 7 days. Each of you names the other on your own phone only; no names or numbers are shared. Unpairing stops everything at once. If you report a friend, we see what they sent you lately so we can act on it.
  • Real weather. Your approximate place, rounded to a square about 25 km across, is sent to our server, which asks MET Norway for the forecast there. It isn't stored with anything that could identify you.
  • The sky tonight, and news from Retkol. Your phone downloads the space station's orbit, the northern-lights forecast, festival dates and new things from our server. Nothing about you is sent.
  • The daily riddle and race. If you answer, your answer is counted with everyone else's, so you can see how many got it. No names are shown.
  • Help improve Retkol. If you turn it on, your phone sends once a day how many times each scene was looked at and touched, as totals with nothing that identifies you.
  • Tell us. Anything you write in the "Tell us" box is sent to us. Please don't include personal details.

Our server keeps no record of your phone's internet address beyond what's needed, for a day, to stop abuse.

Purchases

One scene is free. The others are one-time in-app purchases made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app only checks Google Play's receipt, on your phone, to know which scenes you own. Google's use of your information is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Backup and moving to a new phone

If Android's backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include Retkol's settings and scene progress in your backup, so they come back on a new phone. This is a feature of Android, controlled by you in your phone's settings, and the backup is stored by Google, not by us. Purchases come back from Google Play on their own.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in, email or phone number is ever asked for.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics libraries.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No collection of contacts, files, messages or anything else on your phone. The only photos that ever leave it are the ones you choose to send for your cat.

Children

Retkol is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The online features are off until turned on, and none of them asks for personal details.

Your choices

You can turn location and physical-activity access on or off at any time in your phone's settings, and each online feature on or off in Settings › Online. Unpairing a friend deletes what was waiting between you. Your cat's photos delete themselves (see above). Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your phone. To have anything else we hold deleted (a finished cat coat, say), write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If Retkol adds features that need your data, this policy will be updated before those features arrive, and the app will explain them and leave them off until you turn them on. The date at the top always shows the current version.
